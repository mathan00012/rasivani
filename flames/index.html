How to play FLAMES

  1. Write both names.
  2. Strike out every letter that appears in both names, one for one.
  3. Count the letters that are left.
  4. Write F L A M E S. Count round the word by that number and strike out the letter you land on.
  5. Keep going from the next letter until one is left: Friends, Love, Affection, Marriage, Enemies or Siblings.

RasiVani does exactly this, and shows which letters were struck out. For Tamil, Telugu, Hindi and other scripts, a letter with its vowel sign (like கா or కా) counts as one letter, the way people count by hand.

Where FLAMES comes from

FLAMES is a playground game, not astrology. It spread through schools across India, Sri Lanka, the Philippines and beyond in the 1980s and 90s, on the last page of notebooks. It has no basis in any tradition, which is half the fun.

Questions people ask

What does FLAMES stand for?

Friends, Love, Affection, Marriage, Enemies, Siblings.

Should I use first names or full names?

Either. Most people use first names. Different spellings give different answers, which is part of the game.

Can I play FLAMES in Tamil or Telugu?

Yes. Type both names in the same script so common letters can be struck out.

Is FLAMES a real prediction?

No. It is a counting game for fun. For marriage matching, families use porutham from birth stars.
