Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Your birth details stay on your device

Names, dates, times and places you type are processed only in your browser. They are not sent to our servers or stored by us. Your language, city and chart-style choices are saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers them; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Place search runs on our own offline list first. Only when it has few matches, when you tap “Search more places”, “Pick on map” or “Use my location” does your browser send the place name you typed, or the map point / your location’s coordinates, to OpenStreetMap services (Photon by komoot, Nominatim, and the OpenStreetMap map tiles) to find the place. Your location is asked for only when you tap “Use my location”, and is used only to fill the birth place box.

Paid reports

When you buy a detailed report, the report is still calculated in your browser. To take payment and deliver it we share only what is needed: Razorpay (our payment gateway) receives your email, the amount and a short code made from your birth date, time and place, so a paid report can be reopened later; your card or UPI details go directly to Razorpay and never reach us. The finished PDF is sent to your email through our email provider (Resend) and is not stored by us after sending. Payment records are kept by Razorpay as Indian law requires.
