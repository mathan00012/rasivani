Rasi and Lagnam are not the same thing

VANI proudly says "My lagnam is Mesham" and the Pandit sighs: "That is your rasi, child." Here is the difference in one minute.

  • Rasi (janma rasi) = the sign the Moon occupied at birth. The Moon stays in one sign about 2.25 days.
  • Lagnam (ascendant) = the sign rising on the eastern horizon at birth. It changes roughly every 2 hours.
  • Two babies born the same day in the same city usually share a rasi but can have different lagnams.
  • Daily rasi palan uses the rasi; the houses of the chart are counted from the lagnam.

Rasi: where the Moon was

The zodiac is 12 signs of 30°. The Moon moves about 13° a day, so it spends roughly 2¼ days in each sign. The sign it was in at your birth is your janma rasi, and the nakshatra it was in is your birth star. Newspaper and YouTube rasi palans in South India are read from this Moon sign.

Lagnam: what was rising

As the Earth turns, all 12 signs rise over the eastern horizon once a day. The sign rising at the exact minute and place of birth is the lagnam. Some signs rise faster than others, but on average a new lagnam rises every 2 hours. That is why the chart needs birth time and birth place, not only the date.

How they work together

In a jathagam the lagnam is house 1, and every other house (family, career, marriage and so on) is counted from it. The rasi shows the mind and is used for dasha start, daily transits (gochara) and matching. Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra treats both: the lagna chart and the Chandra (Moon) chart. Questions people ask

Why do my sun sign and rasi differ?

Western sun signs use the Sun, not the Moon, and the tropical zodiac, which is now about 24° ahead of the sidereal zodiac used in India.

If I do not know my birth time?

You can still get your rasi (unless the Moon changed sign that day), but the lagnam will be uncertain.

Can rasi and lagnam be the same sign?

Yes. If the Moon was in the rising sign at birth, both are the same.
