Three ways to draw the same jathagam

VANI shows her Chennai chart to a friend from Lucknow, who turns it sideways and frowns. Nothing is wrong: it is the same sky, drawn in a different style.

  • South Indian (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra, Telangana, Sri Lanka): 12 boxes around the edge; each box is always the same sign, Meenam top-left, Mesham next to it, going clockwise.
  • North Indian (North and West India, Nepal): a diamond; the top-centre house is always house 1 (lagnam) and numbers show which sign is in each house; houses go anticlockwise.
  • East Indian (Bengal, Odisha, Assam, Bangladesh): a 3×3 grid with cut corners; signs are fixed, Mesham top-centre, going anticlockwise.
  • All three hold exactly the same information. Pick the one your family reads.

South Indian: signs stay put

The signs never move. Meenam (Pisces) is the top-left box, Mesham (Aries) the next one to its right, and the signs run clockwise around the square. The lagnam is shown by a mark (often a diagonal line or "La") in whichever box holds it. To read houses, count from that box clockwise.

North Indian: houses stay put

Here the houses are fixed instead. The top-centre diamond is always house 1, and houses run anticlockwise. A number in each house tells you which sign falls there (1 = Mesha, 2 = Vrishabha … 12 = Meena). It is quick for reading houses, but you must read the numbers to know the signs.

East Indian: a third fixed-sign layout

Used in Bengal, Odisha and nearby regions. Signs are fixed again, but Mesha sits in the top-centre and the signs go anticlockwise, with the corner boxes split diagonally to hold two signs each. Our chart page offers all three, with a sample of each before you generate.

Why no Western wheel?

Western astrology mostly uses the tropical zodiac, which today is about 24° ahead of the sidereal (Lahiri) zodiac used in Indian charts. Showing a Western wheel with Indian positions would confuse readers comparing it with Western sites, so we keep to the three Indian styles.

Questions people ask

Which chart style is correct?

All three are correct. They are different drawings of the same positions.

How do I find the lagnam in a North Indian chart?

It is always the top-centre diamond (house 1). The number written there is the lagnam sign.

Can I switch styles after making my chart?

Yes. Pick South, North or East on the chart page and the same chart is redrawn.
