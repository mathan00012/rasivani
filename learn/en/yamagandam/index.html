Yamagandam: Yama's "office hours"

VANI books a meeting at noon on Sunday and Thatha shakes his head: "Yama is at his desk then." What is Yamagandam, and why does its time change with the place and the day?

  • Daytime (sunrise to sunset) is cut into 8 equal parts.
  • Yamagandam part by weekday: Thu 1, Wed 2, Tue 3, Mon 4, Sun 5, Sat 6, Fri 7.
  • It is about 1.5 hours long, shorter in winter, longer in summer.
  • Because sunrise differs by city, Yamagandam in Chennai and Delhi are different clock times.

How it is calculated

Take the local sunrise and sunset for the day. Divide the daytime into 8 equal parts. Yamagandam is one of those parts, and which one depends on the weekday. Notice the pattern: from Thursday it moves one part later each day going backwards through the week (Thu 1, Wed 2, Tue 3, Mon 4, Sun 5, Sat 6, Fri 7). The 8th part is never used for Yamagandam.

Example: if sunrise is 6:00 and sunset 18:00, each part is 90 minutes. On a Sunday the 5th part runs from 12:00 to 13:30.

Rahu kalam, Yamagandam and Gulika

These three come from the same 8-part split of the day, each with its own weekday order. Rahu kalam is linked to the shadow planet Rahu, Yamagandam to Yama, and Gulika (Gulika kalam) to Mandi/Gulika, the son of Saturn described in the upagraha chapter of Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. Traditionally people avoid starting new work in Rahu kalam and Yamagandam; Gulika is treated as a time when whatever you do tends to repeat.

What it is, and what it is not

The calculation is plain astronomy: sunrise and sunset are measured, and the split is arithmetic. Whether a time is lucky or unlucky is tradition and belief, not something science measures. Use it the way families have for centuries, as a guide for when to begin things, not as a reason to fear.

Questions people ask

How long is Yamagandam?

One eighth of the daytime: about 1 h 30 min near the equinox, around 1 h 25 min in a South Indian winter and longer in summer.

Is Yamagandam the same everywhere?

No. It depends on local sunrise and sunset, so it changes with the city and the date. Use the Rahu kalam finder to get it for your place.

Is there a night Yamagandam?

Some almanacs also list night periods using sunset to next sunrise. Most daily panchangams print only the daytime one.
