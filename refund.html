Refund, cancellation and delivery policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

Delivery

All reports are digital. Nothing is shipped. Your report appears on screen right after payment, a PDF downloads to your device, and a copy is emailed to the address you give, usually within 5 minutes. You can reopen a paid report any time on the same device, or on any device with your payment ID and the same birth details.

Refunds

  • Not delivered: if you paid but did not receive your report because of a technical problem, email us within 7 days with your payment ID and we will deliver it or refund the full amount.
  • Charged twice: duplicate payments for the same report are refunded in full.
  • Wrong birth details: if you typed a wrong date, time or place, email us within 7 days and we will send a corrected report once, free.
  • Because a report is generated instantly and cannot be returned, we do not offer refunds for a change of mind once it has been delivered.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days.

Cancellation

You can cancel any time before paying. A failed or cancelled payment is never charged; if money left your account, your bank returns it automatically.
