How the friendship score works

Each date of birth gives that person's birth star (the nakshatra the Moon was in, worked out for midday India time). Then three star rules from the marriage porutham are used, because they are really about how two people get along: tara (count from one star to the other, both ways; Sampat, Kshema, Sadhana, Mitra and Parama Mitra are the friendly counts, up to 30 points), gana (Deva, Manushya or Rakshasa nature, up to 30) and yoni (the animal of each star, up to 25). The names add up to 15 more, and they also pick which cartoon you get, so the same pair always gets the same score and cartoon, in either order.

Belief and fact

Tara, gana and yoni come from traditional Indian astrology. No scientific study has shown that birth stars decide who becomes friends. Friendship grows from time, trust and kindness. Use the score to start a laugh, not to judge anyone.

Questions people ask

Is the friendship score accurate?

It is for fun. It follows traditional star rules (tara, gana, yoni) from your birth dates, plus a fixed number from your names.

Why does the same pair always get the same score?

The score is worked out from the names (and stars), not drawn at random, so you can check it again and show others.

Do you store the names?

No. Everything runs on your phone. The names only leave it if you press Share and send the link yourself.

Why do you need the date of birth?

To find each person's birth star. We use midday India time; for your exact star, make your free jathagam with your birth time and place.

Astrology is a traditional belief system, not science. Use these results as cultural guidance only, never for medical, financial or legal decisions.
