What is yoni?

Yoni is one of the ten poruthams (Yoni porutham, Yoni koota) used when matching horoscopes for marriage. Each of the 27 nakshatras is given one of 14 animals: horse, elephant, sheep, serpent, dog, cat, rat, cow, buffalo, tiger, deer, monkey, mongoose and lion. Two stars with the same animal are thought to get on naturally; seven pairs are natural rivals, such as cat and rat, or serpent and mongoose.

The personality lines here are a friendly reading of each animal, not a classical text. Astrology is a tradition and belief, not science.

Questions people ask

How do I find my birth star?

Your birth star is the nakshatra the Moon was in when you were born. Pick "Use my birth date", or make your free jathagam on RasiVani.

Which stars share my animal?

Most animals have two stars. The mongoose has only one, Uttara Ashadha. The result shows the others with your animal.

What is a natural rival?

Yoni lists seven enemy pairs: horse and buffalo, elephant and lion, sheep and monkey, serpent and mongoose, dog and deer, cat and rat, cow and tiger. In porutham such a pair scores low on yoni.

Is this used in marriage matching?

Yes, yoni is one of the ten poruthams. For a full match use the marriage porutham tool.
