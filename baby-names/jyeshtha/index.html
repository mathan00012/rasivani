Questions people ask

What are the name letters for Jyeshtha nakshatra?

Pada 1: No, pada 2: Ya, pada 3: Yi, pada 4: Yu.

Which rasi does Jyeshtha belong to?

Pada 1: Vrischika (Scorpio), Pada 2: Vrischika (Scorpio), Pada 3: Vrischika (Scorpio), Pada 4: Vrischika (Scorpio)

Which letter should I use for my baby born in Jyeshtha?

Use the letter of the pada the Moon was in at the time of birth. The free birth chart on RasiVani shows the pada.

Choosing the first letter from the birth star is a tradition, not a rule. A name the family loves, that is easy to say and carries a good meaning, matters most.
