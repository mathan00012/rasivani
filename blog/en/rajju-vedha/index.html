Rajju and Vedha Porutham: Why They Matter Most

What Rajju dosham and Vedha dosham mean in marriage matching, which stars fall in each rajju, and why families treat these two poruthams as essential.

Published: 8 November 2026 · RasiVani

Why these two are special

Of the ten poruthams, Rajju and Vedha are usually treated as essential. Many families go ahead with fewer total matches if these two are present, but hesitate when either is missing.

Rajju porutham

The 27 stars are grouped into five rajjus: Pada (feet), Kati (waist), Nabhi (navel), Kanta (neck) and Siro (head). When the bride and groom have stars in the same rajju, it is called rajju dosham and is linked in tradition to the couple’s long life together. Different rajjus mean the porutham is present.

Vedha porutham

Certain pairs of stars are said to obstruct each other, for example Ashwini and Jyeshta. If the couple’s stars form such a pair, Vedha is missing. If not, Vedha porutham is present.
