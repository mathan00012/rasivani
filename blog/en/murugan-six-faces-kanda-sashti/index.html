Why Does Murugan Have 6 Faces? The Six Secrets of Kanda Sashti

Kanda Sashti begins today. Why does Lord Murugan have six faces? Six sparks, the six Krittika mothers of the Pleiades, the six abodes of Tamil Nadu, the demon who became a peacock and a rooster, Murugan from Kataragama to Malaysia's Batu Caves, and how the six-day fast is kept.

The full story

Six faces. Six sacred abodes. Six days of fasting. The number six keeps returning in Murugan's story. Why?

Six sparks, six babies

The Puranas say six sparks of fire came from Shiva's third eye. They fell on six lotuses in the Saravana lake and became six babies. Six Krittika mothers raised them. In the sky, they are the star cluster Krittika, the Pleiades. When Parvati embraced all six, they became one child with six faces. That is Shanmukha, the six-faced one.

The six abodes

In Tamil Nadu, Murugan has six special temples, called the Arupadai Veedu, the six abodes. Each has its own story. At Swamimalai, the boy Murugan explained the meaning of Om to his own father, Shiva. These six shrines are praised in Thirumurugatruppadai, an ancient Tamil poem by Nakkeerar.

Soorasamharam: the peacock and the rooster

On the sixth day comes Soorasamharam. With the Vel, the spear his mother Parvati gave him, Murugan faces Soorapadman. The demon hides as a mango tree. The Vel splits the tree in two. One half became the peacock he rides. The other became the rooster on his flag. He did not destroy. He transformed.

Murugan around the world

Murugan belongs to more than Tamil Nadu. At Kataragama in Sri Lanka, people of several faiths worship him. Malaysia's Batu Caves has a 140-foot statue of Murugan. Singapore and Mauritius celebrate Thaipusam too. In the Telugu states, Subrahmanya Shashti comes in Margashira. In the north, he is Kartikeya.

How people keep the six-day fast

Devotees fast for six days. One meal a day, or only fruit and milk. Every day, they recite the Kanda Sashti Kavasam, written by Devaraya Swamigal. If you have a health condition, keep a lighter fast on your doctor’s advice. Devotion lives in the heart.
