Sade Sati (Ezharai Sani): Meaning, Phases and Simple Remedies

Sade Sati explained: the 7½-year Saturn transit, its three phases, Ashtama and Ardhashtama Shani, which rasis have it now, and calm practical remedies.

Published: 9 November 2026 · RasiVani

What Sade Sati is

Saturn spends about two and a half years in each sign. Sade Sati (Ezharai Sani in Tamil) is the seven and a half years when Saturn moves through the sign before your moon sign, your moon sign itself and the sign after it.

The three phases

The first phase often brings extra expenses and travel, the middle phase tests health and patience, and the last phase concerns money and family speech. Results depend on your full jathagam, so two people with the same rasi can feel it very differently.

Ashtama and Ardhashtama Shani

Saturn in the 8th sign from your rasi is called Ashtama Shani and in the 4th sign Ardhashtama Shani (Kantaka Shani). These are also periods for care. Every RasiVani rasi page shows which of these is running for that rasi today.

Practical remedies

Honest hard work is said to please Saturn most. Many people light a sesame-oil lamp on Saturdays, help elderly or poor people, and pray to Lord Hanuman or Lord Shani. Avoid shortcuts, risky loans and quarrels during this time.
